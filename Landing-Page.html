FAQs
What's included in this coaching?
3 x 30-minute private sessions over Zoom — Portfolio Walkthrough, Market Sizing Deep-Dive, and a Final Mock Pitch. Includes recording, transcript, and personalised action items after every session.
What happens if I need to reschedule?
You can reschedule any session up to 4 hours before the start time, up to 2 times per session. Use the link in your booking email or the reschedule button in your dashboard.
How long do I have access to the recordings?
Recordings are available for 15 days after each session. A download link is also emailed to you immediately after the session ends so you can save a copy.
Do I need to have a deck ready before session 1?
Yes — session 1 is a full walkthrough of your existing deck. Even a rough draft works. The goal is to surface what needs fixing before we start building improvements in sessions 2 and 3.
